Frequently Asked Questions

How much does PenLog cost?

PenLog offers flexible pricing based on your needs. We work with individual pen supervisors, contractors, and fleet operators. Contact us to discuss the best option for your operation. Early access users receive preferred pricing.

Do contractors need to create accounts?

No! Contractors get a simple link. They click it, submit pen status, upload photos, and they're done. No app downloads, no passwords, no training required.

Is our data secure?

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Photos are stored securely. We're GDPR compliant. Your dry dock data is yours—you can export it anytime in standard formats (PDF, Excel).

Does this work offline?

Yes — PenLog is offline-first. Contractors can record a penetration and capture its photos with no signal at all. Everything is saved on the device and syncs automatically the moment a connection returns — nothing is lost, even if the app is closed in between. Built for the reality of steel hulls and dead zones.

Will Flag State surveyors accept PenLog documentation?

PenLog generates documentation that meets SOLAS Chapter II-2 requirements for bulkhead penetrations. Classification Society surveyors (Lloyd's Register, DNV, ABS, etc.) verify compliance during drydock based on the completeness and accuracy of your records - not which tool you used to create them. Our exports include all standard information required: pen location, contractor, opening/closing dates, photos, and verification status. We recommend showing your surveyor a sample export before the drydock to confirm it meets their specific preferences. Every record also carries a system-generated timestamp and named attribution, and can't be altered without the change appearing in the audit trail — the kind of integrity surveyors look for.
